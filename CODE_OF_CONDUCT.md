# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone contributing to **Airgap PDF**, regardless of background or experience level. This applies to all project spaces: repositories, issue trackers, PR comments, chats, and documentation.

All participants must follow the rules and recommendations below. If you do not feel comfortable upholding these principles, please do not contribute.

## The 6 Principles

1. **Be Nice.**
   Treat others as you would like to be treated. Avoid cynicism, sarcasm, and dismissive language.

2. **Be Inclusive.**
   Recognize and value contributions from all backgrounds, skill levels, and perspectives. Acknowledge that people may have different approaches or priorities, and that none is inherently superior.

3. **Be Respectful.**
   Criticize ideas, not people. Be constructive and specific in feedback, and assume positive intent unless evidence suggests otherwise.

4. **Be Patient.**
   Remember that people learn at different paces and may need time to understand complex topics. Offer guidance without judgment.

5. **Be Professional.**
   Use clear, concise communication. Stay on topic and avoid unnecessary distractions. Respect working hours and availability.

6. **Be Trustworthy.**
   Always operate with integrity. Do not misrepresent yourself, your work, or your intentions.

## Guidelines

- **Code of Conduct:** Uphold the spirit and letter of this Code of Conduct in all interactions.
- **Positive Encouragement:** Actively seek opportunities to praise and support others.
- **Constructive Feedback:** When offering criticism, be specific, actionable, and kind.
- **Conflict Resolution:** Address disagreements privately and respectfully first. If a resolution cannot be reached, escalate to a project maintainer.
- **Non-Discrimination:** Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, or any other personal characteristic will not be tolerated.
- **Privacy Respect:** Do not share anyone's personal information without explicit consent.
- **Documentation:** Keep documentation clear, accurate, and accessible.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All reports will be taken seriously and investigated promptly and confidentially.

Maintainers will take whatever action is deemed appropriate to resolve the situation, which may include:
- A private warning or caution.
- A request for a public apology.
- Removal from project channels or communications.
- Temporary or permanent expulsion from the project.

Anyone asked to stop any behavior is expected to comply immediately.
